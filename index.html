Explore Fly Fishing in Argentina

Argentina is a remarkable destination for fly-fishing enthusiasts. In Tierra del Fuego, the Río Grande is internationally known for its sea-run brown trout and unique fishing opportunities.

Its Patagonian landscapes, flowing waters and remote surroundings make the region an exciting destination for anglers seeking a memorable fishing experience.

Discover Sea-Run Brown Trout

Sea-run brown trout migrate between freshwater rivers and the sea. Their size, strength and challenging behaviour make them a sought-after target for fly fishers.

Anglers visiting Río Grande can experience this distinctive fishery while exploring the natural beauty of southern Argentina.

Planning Your Fishing Adventure

Before travelling, research the fishing season, local regulations, weather conditions and recommended fly-fishing equipment.

Warm layers, waterproof clothing and guidance from experienced local professionals can help you prepare for the changing weather of Tierra del Fuego.

Responsible fishing practices and careful fish handling are important for protecting this exceptional fishery.
